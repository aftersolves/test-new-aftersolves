The day-to-day problem

The scheduling problem in clinics and healthcare providers

Healthcare scheduling is a layering of constraints that a paper diary or a shared inbox cannot hold. A single clinic may run three practitioners across two rooms, each with their own working hours, leave and appointment lengths. Patients call to book, reschedule and cancel throughout the day; recall patients need to be contacted months later; no-shows leave gaps that another patient could have used. Front desk staff end up as human schedulers — copying details between a diary, a messaging app and a patient list — and the moment someone is away, the schedule fragments. The cost is not only staff time but missed appointments, double-booked rooms and patients who give up and go elsewhere.

How it fits

How Aftersolves Booking fits clinics and healthcare providers

Aftersolves Booking holds the whole schedule in one place. Each practitioner gets their own availability and service durations, so a 15-minute check-up and a 45-minute procedure book into the right slot automatically. Patients self-serve online at any hour, so overnight requests land on the calendar before the clinic opens. Automated email and SMS reminders cut no-shows, and the customer record keeps contact details, appointment history and notes together — so a recall list is a filter, not a project. When a practitioner is on leave, one change closes their slots everywhere, with no risk of a double booking.

Questions

Questions clinics and healthcare providers businesses ask

Can patients book a specific practitioner?

+

Yes. Each service can be tied to one or more practitioners, and patients can choose a practitioner or accept the next available, depending on how you configure it.

Does it handle different appointment lengths?

+

Yes. You set the duration per service, and the calendar blocks the correct amount of time. A 15-minute check-up and a 45-minute procedure will not overlap on the same practitioner.

Is patient data secure?

+

Booking data is stored on the platform under your account. For clinical or medical records, Aftersolves Booking holds appointment and contact data — you should keep clinical notes in your existing records system.
