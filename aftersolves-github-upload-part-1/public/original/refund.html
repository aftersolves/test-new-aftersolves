Refund & Cancellation Policy

Refund, cancellation and payment reversal conditions applicable to Aftersolves products and services.

Company: Aftersolves

Website: https://www.aftersolves.com/

Contact email: support@aftersolves.com

Governing jurisdiction: Malaysia

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Last updated 7 November 2026

1

Introduction

This policy explains the refund, cancellation and payment reversal conditions applicable to Aftersolves products and services. It applies to:

  • Aftersolves Booking subscriptions.
  • Website development projects.
  • Custom software development.
  • SaaS development.
  • Maintenance services.
  • Digital products and other paid services.

Different refund conditions apply depending on the nature of the purchase.

2

General refund principles

All refund requests are reviewed individually based on:

  • The service purchased.
  • Work already performed.
  • Resources and expenses already committed.
  • Project milestones completed.
  • Subscription usage.
  • Applicable contractual terms.
  • Applicable consumer protection laws.

Payment does not automatically create an unconditional right to a refund. Nothing in this policy removes rights that cannot legally be excluded.

3

Aftersolves Booking subscriptions

Aftersolves Booking operates on a subscription basis.

3.1 Subscription cancellation

Customers may request cancellation according to the cancellation process provided for their subscription. Cancellation generally prevents future renewals but does not automatically reverse payments already processed.

3.2 Subscription refunds

Subscription fees are generally non-refundable once a subscription period has started or the service has been made available. Exceptions may be considered where:

  • A duplicate payment occurred.
  • An incorrect amount was charged.
  • A verified billing error occurred.
  • Aftersolves is unable to provide the purchased service.
  • A refund is required by applicable law.
  • Aftersolves expressly approves an exception.

3.3 Unused subscription periods

Unused days, partial months or unused subscription allowances are not automatically refundable.

3.4 Failed service provision

Where Aftersolves is responsible for a material failure to provide a paid subscription, we may consider an appropriate remedy, including a refund, account credit or other reasonable resolution.

4

Website development projects

Development projects involve time, planning, technical work and resources. Refund eligibility may depend on the project stage.

4.1 Before work begins

If a project is cancelled before work begins, a refund may be considered after deducting any disclosed, applicable administrative or non-recoverable costs.

4.2 After work begins

Once development has started, refunds may be reduced according to work completed, approved milestones, resources committed and non-recoverable expenses.

4.3 After milestone completion

Payments relating to completed and accepted milestones are generally non-refundable, subject to applicable law and the Project Agreement.

4.4 After final delivery

Once the agreed deliverables have been completed and accepted, payments are generally non-refundable except where a refund is required by law or expressly agreed in writing.

5

Custom software and digital solutions

Custom software projects may involve research, planning, UI/UX design, architecture, programming, database development, API integrations, testing and deployment.

Work already performed may be charged even if the client later decides not to continue the project. Third-party expenses, hosting charges, domain registrations and other non-recoverable costs may also be deducted where legally permitted and properly disclosed.

6

Non-refundable circumstances

Subject to applicable law, refunds will generally not be provided for:

  • Change of mind after work has commenced.
  • Failure to provide required client materials.
  • Delays caused by the client’s lack of response.
  • Failure to use a purchased subscription.
  • Unused subscription allowances.
  • Completed and accepted development milestones.
  • Approved additional work already performed.
  • Third-party costs already incurred and not recoverable.
  • Dissatisfaction resulting solely from a change in personal preference after approval.

7

Additional work and revisions

Additional revisions, modifications and new features are separate services where they fall outside the agreed project scope. Payments for approved additional work are generally non-refundable once that work has commenced, subject to applicable law.

8

Refund request procedure

Customers requesting a refund should contact support@aftersolves.com. Please provide:

  • Full name.
  • Registered email address.
  • Invoice or transaction reference.
  • Product or service purchased.
  • Payment date.
  • Reason for the request.
  • Supporting information where applicable.

9

Review and processing

Aftersolves will review refund requests and may request additional information. Approved refunds will generally be returned through the original payment method where possible. Processing times may depend on the payment provider, bank or financial institution.

10

Cancellation of development projects

Clients wishing to cancel a development project must provide written notice. The final amount payable will be determined by:

  • Work completed.
  • Approved milestones.
  • Outstanding invoices.
  • Committed resources.
  • Approved additional work.
  • Recoverable and non-recoverable expenses.
  • The applicable Project Agreement.

Any remaining balance or refund will be calculated accordingly.

11

Chargebacks and payment disputes

Customers should contact Aftersolves first to resolve billing concerns. Unjustified chargebacks or fraudulent payment disputes may result in account restrictions or suspension where permitted by law. This does not restrict customers from exercising legitimate payment dispute rights.

12

Policy updates

We may update this policy when necessary. Current version: https://www.aftersolves.com/refund.
