Mory Coffee

A client website designed to showcase Mory Coffee and create a more engaging online experience for its customers.

ClientMory Coffee
DisciplineWebsite Design & Development
Live sitemory.ddnet.my

Overview

A storefront that feels like the brand.

Mory Coffee came to Aftersolves for a website that would do justice to the brand — not a generic template, but a storefront that reflects the care they put into every cup. The result is a responsive, fast-loading website built around their identity, their menu, and the way their customers actually discover and engage with the business online.

The build focused on three things: a clean visual system that lets the product photography lead, a responsive layout that works from phone to desktop, and a content structure that makes it easy for the team at Mory Coffee to keep the site current as the menu and story evolve.

What we shipped

Scope of the engagement.

  • Custom website design and front-end development.
  • Responsive layout across mobile, tablet and desktop.
  • Brand-aligned visual system — typography, colour, spacing.
  • Content structure built for easy updates by the client team.
  • Performance and SEO baseline configuration.
