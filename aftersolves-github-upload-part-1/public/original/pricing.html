What does the monthly subscription include?

Aftersolves Booking subscriptions include online booking, customer management, staff schedules and booking notifications. Growth adds reservations, queues, reminders, analytics, multi-branch management and website embedding eligibility. The exact feature set depends on the selected plan.

Do you charge per staff member?

No. We do not charge per seat. Adding a teammate should never cost you money. Plans differ by feature availability and branch limits, not by headcount.

How is website development priced?

Website development is one-off and billed against an approved quotation or Project Agreement. Starter Sites begin from RM 2,500 and Growth Sites from RM 6,500; Custom Builds are quoted individually. A typical schedule includes an initial deposit, milestone payments and a final delivery payment. See our Payment Policy for the full framework.

Do you offer ongoing website management?

Yes. After a one-off build you can add Monthly Managed by Aftersolves, covering hosting management, routine maintenance, security and compatibility monitoring, troubleshooting, minor content updates within your allowance and technical support. Monthly fees are quoted separately based on your website. Management does not include unlimited changes or additional development — those are quoted on request.

Is there a minimum contract period?

Booking Starter and Growth are month-to-month. Enterprise and Custom development projects are governed by the applicable Project Agreement, which may include annual commitments.

Are hosting and third-party services included?

Unless expressly included in a quotation or subscription plan, hosting, domains, databases, third-party services and infrastructure are billed separately. We always disclose these costs upfront.

Can I change plans mid-month?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your subscription at any time. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle. Downgrades may be subject to the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept online payment gateways, bank transfers, DuitNow and other supported payment methods. Payments are considered received when the payment provider or financial institution confirms successful processing.
