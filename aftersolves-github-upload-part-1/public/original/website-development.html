How we work

A clear, eight-step development process

Every project follows a documented process so you always know what is happening and what comes next.

01

Initial Enquiry

You submit your project requirements through our contact form or quote request.

02

Consultation

Aftersolves reviews your business needs, goals and constraints with you.

03

Proposal & Quotation

We prepare scope, pricing and an estimated timeline for the project.

04

Project Confirmation

You approve the proposal and the payment arrangement is finalized.

05

Design & Development

Work begins according to the agreed scope, with regular check-ins.

06

Review & Testing

The solution is reviewed with you and tested across devices and use cases.

07

Deployment

The completed solution is deployed to production according to the agreement.

08

Maintenance & Support

Ongoing support may be provided under a separate arrangement.

Important project conditions

What every project includes

To keep expectations clear from day one, the following conditions apply to every website development project:

  • Every project has an agreed scope documented in a Project Agreement.
  • Project pricing depends on requirements and complexity.
  • Additional features may require additional fees.
  • Revisions outside the agreed scope may incur charges.
  • Hosting, domains and third-party services may be charged separately.
  • Delivery timelines depend on project requirements and client cooperation.
  • Ownership and licensing are governed by the applicable Project Agreement.
  • For managed services, infrastructure and backend systems may remain under Aftersolves’ control.

See our Terms & Conditions and Payment Policy for the full framework.
