About

Shawal leads the marketing and communications team at Aftersolves. He owns the strategic vision for the brand and drives growth and engagement across our digital channels.

His focus is on campaigns and data-driven strategies that elevate Aftersolves' market presence and connect with the audience effectively — without overpromising what the software can do.

Shawal works closely with the product and engineering teams to make sure everything we put into market is accurate, current, and grounded in what we actually ship.

Story

Shawal's remit is built on a simple rule the whole company follows: we don't market features that don't exist. That makes the marketing team's job harder in the short term and far more credible in the long term.

He has shaped the brand voice around respect — for the product, for the customer's time, and for the engineering team's work. Campaigns are planned against what the product actually delivers, and claims are checked against the roadmap before they go out.

Day to day, Shawal runs the content calendar, oversees the digital channels, and works with leadership on positioning — making sure the story Aftersolves tells in public matches the work happening inside.

“Marketing that respects the product. We don't promise what the software can't deliver.”
