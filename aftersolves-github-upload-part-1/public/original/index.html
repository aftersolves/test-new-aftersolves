Contact us

Is Aftersolves a website design agency?

No. Aftersolves is both a software product provider and a professional website and software development service provider. Our two primary offerings are Aftersolves Booking, a subscription platform, and Website Development, a professional service.

What does Aftersolves Booking cost?

Aftersolves Booking is subscription-based, with monthly or annual billing and plan-specific features. Pricing is presented during the subscription process. We do not charge per seat — adding staff should never cost you more.

Do you charge per staff member on Aftersolves Booking?

No. We do not charge per seat. Adding a teammate should never cost you money.

How is website development priced?

Website development is billed according to an approved quotation or Project Agreement. Pricing depends on requirements and complexity. A typical schedule includes an initial deposit, milestone payments and a final delivery payment.

Who owns the website or software you build for us?

Ownership and licensing are governed by the applicable Project Agreement. Unless a separate written agreement expressly provides otherwise, Aftersolves retains ownership of its proprietary platforms, reusable components and infrastructure. Clients receive the usage rights expressly described in their agreement.

Do you offer managed hosting?

Yes. Certain websites and applications may be developed, hosted, operated or maintained under Aftersolves’ management. For managed services, infrastructure may remain under Aftersolves control and access is provided according to the agreed service level.

Can I get a custom domain for my Aftersolves Booking site?

Yes. Custom domains and website embedding are available on eligible plans. We can guide you through the DNS configuration if your domain is registered elsewhere.

What happens if I want changes after my website is delivered?

Additional edits, revisions, modifications and new features outside the agreed project scope may incur fees. We always obtain your approval before beginning chargeable additional work.
